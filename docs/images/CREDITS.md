# Credits for /docs/images

The images in this folder illustrate the AVDBCMS page on GitHub; AVDBCMS itself never loads them. AvdbAPI.com made
them for AVDBCMS, and like the rest of docs/ they are covered by the AVDBCMS Community License 1.0 (/LICENSE, Part
A.1), except for the third-party parts named below, which keep their own licences.

## Artwork (SVG): hero, feature icons, badges

- All drawings are original and made for AVDBCMS: the AVDBCMS mark (a video database with a play button), the
  browser window and its posters, the feature icons and the badge icons. No third-party logo is used: the PHP,
  database, GitHub, X and Telegram badges carry generic symbols (code brackets, a database, a star, a share graph, a
  paper plane). PHP, MySQL, MariaDB, Laravel, GitHub, X and Telegram are trademarks of their owners and are named only
  to say what AVDBCMS runs on or where to find it.
- Text inside the SVG files is converted to outlines from Outfit (Copyright 2021 The Outfit Project Authors), Plus
  Jakarta Sans (Copyright 2020 The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors) and Playfair Display (Copyright 2017 The
  Playfair Display Project Authors, with Reserved Font Name "Playfair Display"), all under the SIL Open Font License
  1.1. Outfit and Plus Jakarta Sans are the fonts of the default AVDBCMS theme; Playfair Display is the display font
  of the Silver Screen theme. No font file is included here.
- The colours are those of the "AVDBCMS Coral" colour style of the default theme (coral #FF3D61, orange #FF8B5C and
  cyan #18C8D8). The 32 swatches in the hero are the real brand colours of the 32 AVDBCMS themes, taken from the
  theme catalogue of AVDBCMS 1.2.0 (the dark hero shows each theme's dark colour, the light hero its light colour).
- The SVG files hold no script, no link and no outside reference, so they look the same everywhere.

## Screenshots (screenshots/) and the looks wall

- AVDBCMS 1.2.0 on a local demo install with demo content only: generated flat illustration posters, made-up titles,
  channels and members, and example addresses such as example.com. They were taken for the help center at
  help.avdbapi.com, where the same pictures illustrate the guides. The numbered markers on them belong to the
  help-center captions; the README captions list the same numbers.
- The looks wall (demo-looks-dark.webp and demo-looks-light.webp) puts screenshots of the 12 complete looks on that
  demo site side by side, each in the mode it opens in. Its labels are drawn with Outfit and Plus Jakarta Sans (see
  above).
- The WebP files carry no metadata (no EXIF or XMP).
